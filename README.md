# PurpleFoxxx

A four-member cybersecurity collective. We play CTFs, build the challenges
other people play, and run the infrastructure that keeps a competition
standing for a few hundred competitors at once.

Not just players. Builders and operators too.

## What we do

**Play CTFs** — forensics, reversing, web, OSINT, crypto — individually and
as a team, across the college and community CTF circuit.

**Develop challenges** — CTF challenges and Boot2Root machines, designed and
built from the ground up, not just solved.

**Build labs & run infra** — full lab environments (Linux, Windows, Active
Directory, web app, mobile APK) and the hosting/networking backbone that
keeps a competition running. We've also delivered tool-familiarization
training for faculty alongside lab builds.
